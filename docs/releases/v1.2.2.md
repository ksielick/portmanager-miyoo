**New games**

Five new ports, all free and complete, with nothing to add:

- **Frozen Bubble** (Puzzle games): shoot coloured bubbles to clear the board.
- **Panel Attack** (Puzzle games): swap blocks to make lines, with endless, puzzle and time-attack modes, and play against the CPU.
- **Tennis Vs Zombies** (Arcade): fight off zombie hordes with a tennis racket.
- **Mines of Moria** (RPG): a classic roguelike. Go down through the mines and face the Balrog at the bottom.
- **Monk Tower** (RPG): a short roguelike. Climb 20 levels of a tower to get back a lost manuscript.

Press **X** in Port Manager to see and install them.

**What's new**

- **Much faster installs.** A game is no longer written to the SD card twice: once unpacked, it goes straight into place. Panel Attack's last step used to take 30 seconds and now takes 3.
- **All ports A-Z.** The full list (**X**) is now in alphabetical order. Press **Select** there to group it by category, and again to go back.
- **Game status.** Games that are still rough or are a test version from their author say so on their page. Press **Select** on a game's page to see who made the port and its status.
- **Clearer removing.** Removing a game shows a progress bar and a short, plain message when it's done.

**Fixed**

- Games that come with their shortcut marked "not found yet" now show up in the Ports list right after installing.
- A game with no release yet no longer offers **A: check** in its details, and no longer says its files are "OK" when it needs files from your own copy.

**Install for the first time:** download `PortManager-Miyoo.zip`, then copy its `App` and `Roms` folders to your SD card. See the [instructions](https://github.com/ksielick/portmanager-miyoo#install-first-time).
**Already installed:** press **Y** in Port Manager and accept the update.

Copyrighted files (ROMs, game data) are never downloaded or hosted.
