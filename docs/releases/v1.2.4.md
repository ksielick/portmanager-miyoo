**New game**

- **Mario Kart 64 (mr-finster)** (Racing): mr-finster's port of Mario Kart 64, from the same author as the other N64 games here (game menu, save states, sleep and wake). It needs your own Mario Kart 64 (USA) cartridge dump; the game's page says where it goes.

**Two versions of one game**

Mario Kart 64 now comes in two versions, and you can have both:

- **Mario Kart 64**: cacuracaptors' port, as before.
- **Mario Kart 64 (mr-finster)**: the new one.

Each has its own folder, its own entry in the Ports list and its own saves. Installing, updating or removing one never touches the other.

**Install for the first time:** download `PortManager-Miyoo.zip`, then copy its `App` and `Roms` folders to your SD card. See the [instructions](https://github.com/ksielick/portmanager-miyoo#install-first-time).
**Already installed:** press **Y** in Port Manager and accept the update. The new game shows up under **X** after that.

Copyrighted files (ROMs, game data) are never downloaded or hosted.
