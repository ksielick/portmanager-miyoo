**Safer**

- **Checks free space first.** Before installing or updating, Port Manager checks that your SD card has enough room. If it doesn't, it stops and tells you how much space is needed. Nothing gets changed.
- **Never touches anything outside the Ports folders.** Port Manager only writes to and deletes from the Ports list folders, its own backups and its own folder. Your saves, ROMs for other systems, BIOS files, Onion and other apps can't be changed by it, even by mistake. Anything outside those folders is refused and noted in the log.
- **Safer restores.** A backup is only restored if all of its files belong in the Ports folders.

**Fixed**

- A game no longer shows an update when you already have that version (for example "1.0.1" and "v1.0.1").

**Already installed:** press **Y** in Port Manager and accept the update.

Copyrighted files (ROMs, game data) are never downloaded or hosted.
