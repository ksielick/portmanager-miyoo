**New: your backups at a glance (Select)**

Before every update Port Manager backs up what it replaces and the game's saves to `Backups/PortManager/`. On a card with many N64 games these backups can take about 1 GB. Press **Select** on the main list to see them:

- Every backup, also of games you have removed, with its size, and the total at the top.
- **A: compact** one backup, or **Y** all of them: Port Manager leaves out log files and the large data files that N64 games build again from your ROM (up to 53 MB each). Your saves stay. Backups from the first versions, kept as folders, become single files.
- **X: delete** a backup you no longer need.

Port Manager never removes a backup on its own: you decide.

**Smaller new backups**

- New backups of N64 games leave out those large data files and the logs, so they are much smaller. Your saves are still in them.
- Settings files listed for a game (such as Quake's `tyrquake.opt`) are now backed up too. Before, only folders were.
- Port Manager's own backup no longer includes `tokens.txt` (your GitHub tokens) or its log.

**Long names scroll:** a name too long for its row scrolls while it is selected, so you can read it whole.

**Changed:** Select on the main list now opens your backups. Port Manager's own update is offered under **Y**, as the first step of the check.

**Install for the first time:** download `PortManager-Miyoo.zip`, then copy its `App` and `Roms` folders to your SD card. See the [instructions](https://github.com/ksielick/portmanager-miyoo#install-first-time).
**Already installed:** press **Y** in Port Manager and accept the update.

Copyrighted files (ROMs, game data) are never downloaded or hosted.
