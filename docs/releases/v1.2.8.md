**New games from Onion's Ports Collection**

Six free games are now in Port Manager: **C-Dogs** and **OpenTyrian** (Action), **Cave Story** (Platform), **Wizznic** and **Dinothawr** (Puzzle games), and **Hocoslamfy** (Reflex). They come from Onion's [Ports Collection](https://github.com/OnionUI/Ports-Collection) and need no files from you. If you already installed them with Onion's Package Manager, Port Manager finds them and can take them over: your saves and settings stay.

**Your saves stay yours**

Some games come with empty high-score or settings files. Installing or updating such a game over your copy now keeps your own saves, high scores and settings instead of the empty ones.

**Better progress while installing**

- The unpacking bar now counts the files of every download. Before, some games showed "0/1 files" until they were done.
- Updating a game that is already on the card shows a "Checking files" step while Port Manager compares the files, instead of a bar stuck at 100%.

**Clearer messages when a game can't be checked**

Instead of "no release info", Port Manager now says why: the game isn't on GitHub, your GitHub token has no access to it, the token is wrong or expired, or GitHub's limit of checks per hour is used up.

**Install for the first time:** download `PortManager-Miyoo.zip`, then copy its `App` and `Roms` folders to your SD card. See the [instructions](https://github.com/ksielick/portmanager-miyoo#install-first-time).
**Already installed:** press **Y** in Port Manager and accept the update.

Copyrighted files (ROMs, game data) are never downloaded or hosted.
